### `> engineering_principles`

- **Understand before abstracting** — Master underlying mechanics before introducing high-level wrappers.
- **Prefer simple architectures** — Favor minimal dependencies, transparent data flow, and predictable failure states.
- **Build small, testable components** — Design isolated units that do one job reliably and verify with tests.
- **Measure before optimizing** — Rely on profiling and benchmarks rather than intuition.
- **AI as a development tool, not an understanding substitute** — Leverage AI for rapid scaffolding and exploratory prototyping, while rigorously reviewing and understanding every line committed.
